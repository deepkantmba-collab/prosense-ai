Each bar: how often the model is right and doesn't miss real risks. Higher = better.

Layman Translation: What Do These Numbers Mean?
Precision — When we raise an alert, how often is it a real problem? (91% = 9 in 10 alerts are real)
Recall — Of all actual risks, how many do we catch? (88% = we don't miss many)
F1 Score — The combined score. Balanced view of both above.

Known Challenges & Honest Limitations
An academic panel expects you to own your limitations. Hiding them looks worse than naming them.
RESOLVED — VADER Failure
VADER misclassified workplace language ("I go with the model" → positive). Built custom NLP engine instead. 420 phrases, seed=42, 0 mismatches.
PENDING — Class Imbalance
67.1% at-risk vs 32.9% healthy — ratio 2.04:1. Step 12 needs SMOTE or class_weight="balanced" to avoid biased classifier.
PENDING — Multicollinearity
psysafe_score ↔ outcome_score r=0.914. VIF analysis needed before Step 12 — one feature likely needs to be dropped.
CRITICAL — Fully Synthetic Data
No real org signals used. Models trained on synthetic patterns may not generalise to real teams. Biggest production risk before any enterprise deployment.
WEAK NLP — WLB, Job Sat, AI Adoption
r=0.305 (WLB), 0.460 (Job Sat), 0.469 (AI Adoption). Language signals too indirect for these pillars. Phrase redesign planned.
PARTIAL — BERT Fear Index in Canary Only
v0.9.2 at 25% canary. SHAP explainability and bias audit pending before full production rollout.
BLOCKING — Privacy & GDPR Framework Not Formalised
Cannot deploy to real users until consent framework, data residency, and audit logging are defined. Privacy by Design is a design principle here — not yet an implemented system.
Current status: PoC — demonstrable to design partners and academic panels. Not MVP. Next step to MVP: complete Step 12 + connect one real data source + define consent framework.
